Executive summary
Meta brings the volume, Google brings the buyers — and the budget split follows the search demand.

Across 134 days Eternal Wellness invested $27,832 for 208 tracked conversions at a blended CPA of $133.81. Meta took 83.8% of the budget and returned 88% of the conversions.

Google is deliberately small — $33.61/day against Meta's $174.09/day — because the search demand is not there. In Traverse City the Emface keyword set averages about 30 searches a month and the CO2 laser terms about 40. Search - Emface already holds 51.8% impression share and still sees only 32 impressions a day; more budget cannot buy queries nobody is typing, which is why the Helix CO2 campaign sits paused.

Meta does the prospecting instead, at 184 leads to Google's 24. Both walk people through the door at about the same rate, but Google's leads are the ones who buy: 16.7% became paying guests against Meta's 10.3%, worth $350 of booked revenue per lead against $189, at 1.86× ROAS against 1.49×.

How this was measured. Ad leads were matched to Zenoti guest records on normalised first + last name — three spelling variants accepted, three near-misses with different surnames rejected, and leads from any other source excluded. Revenue is Zenoti Sales (inc. tax) booked on sale date between Apr 24 and Sep 4, so packages bought earlier can deliver inside the window and leads generated late in it have not had time to buy. Zero-dollar consults, follow-ups, waived fees and gift-card issuances are kept in the detail, which is why 51 guests transacted but only 23 spent anything. The ROAS is a period snapshot, not a cohort measurement.
